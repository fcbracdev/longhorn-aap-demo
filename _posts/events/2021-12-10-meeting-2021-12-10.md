## MUNITIONS REMOVAL ACTIVITIES AT THE FORMER LONGHORN ARMY AMMUNITION PLANT, LHAAP-17

October 11 - December 10, 2021

The US Army will be implementing a Time Critical Removal Action to address the imminent and substantial threat of suspected military munitions items within LHAAP-17 (TNT burn area) at the former Longhorn Army Ammunition Plant. There will be periodic disposal of military munitions which detonations may be heard and we ask the community to not be alarmed. Munitions removal work at LHAAP-17 is expected to last through November and potentially into December 2021. Should you have any questions regarding this action please contact: Aaron Williams, (918) 629-7925.
