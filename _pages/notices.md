* The Restoration Advisory Board (RAB) approved a change In RAB meeting frequency during the January 2022 RAB meeting. The frequency was changed from four to three times annually. The RAB meeting will be held in February, June and November of every year. 

* The RAB Meeting planned for November 13, 2025 from 5:00pm to 6:00pm at the Karnack Community Center was Canceled due to the government shutdown. The RAB meeting has been rescheduled to March 12, 2026 from 2:00pm to 3:00pm at the Karnack Community Center. A site visit will be conducted following the RAB meeting from 3:00pm to 5:00pm.
